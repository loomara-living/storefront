03

The care ritual

Keep softness
in the details.

  1. 01

    Wash with care

    Follow each product's care label. A gentle cycle and mild detergent are usually the kindest place to begin.

  2. 02

    Dry gently

    Use the temperature shown on the care label and remove promptly to help ease deep creasing.

  3. 03

    Store with room

    Keep clean, fully dry linens in a cool space where air can circulate.
